## Code change and invariants

`native/iop_probe/spu_idle_voices.h` still implements a deliberately bounded
**zero-sample** device model, not general SPU audio:

- Accepts zero sample words with known ADPCM predictors 0–4 and shifts 0–15.
  With zero initial decoder history, these blocks produce zero PCM; histories stay zero.
  Reserved flag bits, noise, nonzero sample words and volume sweeps remain unsupported.
- Ordinary blocks advance NAX into the next block without inventing ENDX or a loop.
- LOOP/START updates LSA unless the guest explicitly wrote LSA after key-on.
- LOOP/END sets ENDX and redirects NAX to LSA + 1; without REPEAT, ADSR stops.
- Existing sample-clock, key-off and `0x0700` zero-loop behaviour is retained.
- Structured key-on/stream rejection diagnostics identify the failing core, voice,
  addresses and decoder state. These do not change the device outcome.

`native/iop_probe/spu_idle_voice_tests.h` covers core 1 voice 23, ordinary block
boundaries, automatic/manual LSA precedence, repeating and non-repeating endpoints,
key-off, RAM mutation, nonzero next-block rejection and noise rejection. Tests use
retired register accesses and sample-clock boundaries, not source-text assertions.
The decode lookahead matters: at pitch `0x1000`, the first block boundary occurs at
sample 13 and the second at sample 41, not samples 7 and 14.

The flag/address behaviour was checked against pinned PCSX2 v2.8.2
[UpdateBlockHeader / DecodeSamples](https://github.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/blob/v2.8.2/pcsx2/SPU2/Mixer.cpp)
and [voice start/address handling](https://github.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/blob/v2.8.2/pcsx2/SPU2/spu2sys.cpp).
The project change is original code; no upstream source is vendored by this commit.
